The **Lottery** randomly rewards four players every **7–9 hours**.

You do not need to buy a ticket or sign up. Simply being **online** makes you eligible for a reward.

## How It Works

1. Every **7–9 hours**, four random players receive rewards.
2. Each winner is selected from a different **level bracket**.
3. No ticket or registration is required.
4. You only need to be **online** when the lottery takes place.
5. **3 minutes before the lottery**, information about the upcoming lottery is displayed.

## Desirable rewards

Up to level 300 the best rewards are Bonus Points and Crowbar (required to crack the Trunks Sword quest).
In the bracket 301-600, the most desirable rewards are random Mistic Weapon and Armor boxes, which can yield lucky winners some of the best equipment available.
