Tailoring allows certain equipment and weapons to be cut using scissors to receive various materials, most notably pieces of cloth.

To learn how to craft burning torch to unlock Huge, please refer to the Huge Quest.
## Scissors

On **Beginners Island**, Scissors can be:
- picked up from the ground after each server restart after burning of a dry bushes;
- received as a level-up reward (50 at level 30).

Scissors are **not sold** by NPC Helper.
Scissors have a **10% chance to break when used**.

### Materials Conversion

**100 Piece of Cloth of the same color** can be converted into **1 Cloth** of that color by clicking on them. Resulting cloth can be separated to 100 pieces after another click.
