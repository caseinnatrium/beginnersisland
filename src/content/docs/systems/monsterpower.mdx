## Monster Power
The higher monster power is, it gives more experience and better loot.
Monster's power can be checked by using **look** to inspect the monster.

| Power | Power Increase | Chance of Occurrence | Experience & Loot |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **1** | +0% | 45% | 1.00× |
| **2** | +5% | 16% | 1.05× |
| **3** | +10% | 10% | 1.10× |
| **4** | +15% | 8% | 1.15× |
| **5** | +20% | 6% | 1.20× |
| **6** | +25% | 5% | 1.25× |
| **7** | +30% | 4% | 1.30× |
| **8** | +35% | 3% | 1.35× |
| **9** | +40% | 2% | 1.40× |
| **10** | +50% | 1% | 1.50× |

## Monster Variants

There are special monster variants that can appear when a monster spawns. They can be distinguished by a skull that is displayed next to their sprite.

| Skull | Type | Spawn Chance | Experience | Loot | Melee & Ki Damage | Health | Task Kills | Bonus Points |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| <img src="http://dblots.org.pl/pictures/icons/skull_white.png" alt="White Skull" /> | Strong | 1 in 200 | 5× | 5× | 1.5× | 3× | 2% | 1 |
| <img src="http://dblots.org.pl/pictures/icons/skull_yellow.png" alt="Yellow Skull" /> | Rich | 1 in 600 | 2× | 2× (5–10× for trade items) | 1× | 5× | 2% | 1 |
| <img src="http://dblots.org.pl/pictures/icons/skull_red.png" alt="Red Skull" /> | Elite | 1 in 1,000 | 100× | 50× | 2× | 300× | 5% | 5 |

Number of bonus points for the monsters specified in the talbe reflects situation on Beginners Island. Depending on monster level, strong monsters give up to 5, and elite up to 10 BP.
Loot boosters do not affect additional trade item drops for rich monsters. Rich monsters do not flee.
For elite monsters, loot drops will be looted no matter if you have them blacklisted in the auto-loot.
Monster variants cannot be spawned by using summoning items, they can only appear via normal respawn mechanic.

## Treasure Boxes

Elite monsters drop Treasure Boxes depending on their strength. The number of boxes that can drop at once is determined by the strength of an Elite monster: weak monster will drop up to 1 box, average up to 2, and the strong up to 3.
Treasure Boxes must be opened using a **Treasure Key**.
Elite monster loot is shared between the players who dealt the most damage to the monster.

The **Basic Treasure Box** is currently the only Treasure Box obtainable on **Beginners Island**. This is the box that can be obtained from monsters that inhabit planet Earth and Namek.

A Basic Treasure Box requires a **Weak Treasure Key** to open. A Weak Treasure Key can be exchanged for **500 Bonus Points**.
