The **Easter Event** is a seasonal event featuring Easter Packs, Easter Bosses, Water Pistols, and various event rewards. Historically, it has been the event that introduced some of the most desirable and strongest pieces of equipment to Beginners Island. Due to subsequent changes to the event, those items are no longer obtainable on the island.

The availability of Easter Event content on **Beginners Island** has changed over the years. The information below is based on event descriptions and details found on the DBLOTS forums.

## When Does the Easter Event Occur?

The Easter Event typically takes place around **Easter**, usually starting in **late March or early April** and continuing for several weeks.

Based on available historical data, the event has started as early as **March 26** and as late as **April 20**. In recent years, the start has generally been between **late March and mid-April**.

The exact duration varies from year to year, and some event activities may have different end dates from the main part of the event.

## Easter Packs

Easter Packs are obtained by killing monsters. Their availability and contents have changed between events.

### 2014 Easter Packs

In 2014, every monster in DBL could drop one of five types of Easter Pack. The chance to obtain each pack was:

| Easter Pack | Drop chance | Contents |
|---|---:|---|
| **Violet Easter Pack** | 0.5% | Water Case; Small Diamond ×25; Dust ×5; Mechanoid Armor; 50 Zenni Bills |
| **Green Easter Pack** | 0.4% | 50 Zenni Bills; Mistic Legs; Saiyan Armor; Saiyan Legs; Saiyan Glover; Short Sword |
| **Blue Easter Pack** | 0.3% | 2 Credit Cards; Green Flask; Rake; Salamandre Skin |
| **Red Easter Pack** | 0.1% | 1, 2, 3, or 5 Easter Eggs |
| **Colorful Easter Pack** | 0.08% | Teddy Bear; Easter Bunny Teddy; Kame Sennin Doll; Goku Doll |

Opening an Easter Pack awarded one randomly selected item from the corresponding pack. The individual loot chances were weighted, with rarer and more valuable items having lower chances of being obtained.

In addition to Easter Packs, **Easter Eggs** could be obtained directly from monsters with a **0.3% chance**, from Red Easter Packs, by winning events organized by the dedicated **Event Maker**, and from eggs periodically scattered across several locations on Earth.

Easter Eggs could be handed in to the **Easter Bunny** in exchange for event rewards. These included temporary bonuses such as **Double EXP**, **Double Loot**, and **+10%–20% Training Boost**, as well as a chance to obtain the **Easter Bunny Teddy**. The chance of receiving the teddy depended on the number of Easter Eggs handed in.

### 2015 Easter Packs

In 2015, the type of Easter Pack dropped depended on the experience value of the monster:

| Monster experience | Possible Easter Packs |
|---|---|
| Up to 1,200 | Violet Easter Pack |
| Up to 7,500 | Violet Easter Pack, Green Easter Pack |
| Up to 30,000 | Green Easter Pack, Blue Easter Pack |
| Up to 120,000 | Blue Easter Pack, Red Easter Pack |
| Above 120,000 | Blue Easter Pack, Colorful Easter Pack |

At the time, the game did not have the **Elite monster system**. As a result, monsters providing more experience than Pandorian could not spawn on Beginners Island, making **Violet Easter Packs the only obtainable pack type** on the island.

The contents and chances of each pack were:

| Easter Pack | Possible contents |
|---|---|
| **Violet** | 50 Zeni Bills (40%), Small Diamond ×25 (30%), Dust ×5 (17%), Water Case (8%), Mechanoid Armor (5%) |
| **Green** | 75 Zeni Bills (50%), Saiyan Armor (15%), Saiyan Legs (15%), Mistic Legs (10%), Saiyan Glover (5%), Short Sword (5%) |
| **Blue** | Green Flask (32%), Rake (32%), 2 Credit Cards (26%), Salamandre Skin (10%) |
| **Red** | 3 Credit Cards (30%), 250 Bonus Points (30%), 30 Hell Fruits (25%), Salamandre Skin (15%) |
| **Colorful** | Purple Rune (30%), Red Rune (30%), Blue Rune (30%), Easter Bunny Teddy (8%), Bear (2%) |

Opening an Easter Pack awarded a randomly selected item from its respective loot pool.
### Later Events

The Easter Pack system was subsequently changed. In some years, packs dropped according to the number of monsters killed rather than the experience value of individual monsters.

The contents also changed over time. Earlier versions could contain a wide range of items, including weapons and equipment. From 2019 onward, the packs were weakened and no longer provided weapons or equipment.

## Easter Bosses

From 2023 onward, **Easter Bosses** appeared on existing monster respawns.

On Beginners Island, they can therefore only appear in areas where suitable monsters already spawn, such as areas containing **Pandorian** and **Commando**.

The largest concentration is in the **Pandorian forest**, due to the greater number of monster spawns there.

The only Easter Boss available on Beginners Island is the **White Egg**.

## Water Pistols

**Water Pistols** are an Easter Event item that can be used to splash other players.

On **Wet Monday (Easter Monday)**, splashing another player can award **1 Bonus Point** to the player who was splashed.

Water Pistols are available on Beginners Island.

## Easter Flowers

Easter Flowers can normally be exchanged through an event NPC.

On Beginners Island, this exchange was not available in the years where the required NPC was absent from the island.

## Easter Backpack

The **Easter Backpack** can be assembled from pieces obtained from Easter Packs.

A total of **100 pieces** are required to complete the backpack.

Although the pieces can be obtained on Beginners Island, collecting enough pieces is practically impossible on Rookgaard due to the large number required.

## Beginners Island Limitations

The availability of Easter Event content on Beginners Island is primarily affected by the island's limited monster pool and the absence of event NPCs.

In particular:

- Monsters with sufficiently high experience to drop higher-tier Easter Packs may not be available.
- Event NPCs required for exchanging Easter Flowers may not be present.
- Easter Bosses can only spawn where suitable existing monster respawns are located.
- Items requiring very large numbers of drops, such as the Easter Backpack, may technically be obtainable but are practically impossible to complete on Rookgaard.
