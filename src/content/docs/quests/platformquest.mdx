The **Platform Quest** is an item-exchange activity located on the **eastern side of the island**.

Place the required items on the designated fields and pull the **lever** to receive a reward. The reward depends on the item set used.

There are currently **3 known item sets**.

## Item Set 1 — Triceratops

### Requirements

Each pull requires:

| Item | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Triceratops Skin | 1 |
| Triceratops Tusk | 3 |

Both items are stackable. Up to **100 of each item** can be placed on the platform, and the required amount is automatically consumed with each pull.

With 100 Skins and 100 Tusks, you can make **33 pulls**, with 1 Tusk remaining.

### Rewards

| Reward | Chance | Effect |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 2× Triceratops | **72.14%** | Summons 2 Triceratops monsters next to you. They can be summoned repeatedly as long as there is a valid space for them to stand. They cannot be summoned on the item placement spot. They are always level 1 and cannot be summoned as strong, rich, or elite variants. |
| Experience | **13.58%** | Grants **600 × your current player level** experience points. |
| Hand Thorns | **14.08%** | — |
| Light Band | **0.10%** | — |
| Tapion Sword | **0.01%** | — |
| Steel Katana | **0.05%** | — |
| Power Glover | **0.01%** | — |
| Red Blast | **0.01%** | — |
| Violet Ball | **0.01%** | — |

> **Triceratops summons:** You can continue summoning Triceratops repeatedly. The only limitation is that there must be a valid space available for the monsters to stand. They do not use or block the fields where items are placed.

> **Experience:** The experience reward is calculated as **600 × current player level**. For example, a player at level 10 receives **6,000 experience points**.

## Item Set 2 — Kastets

### Requirements

Each pull requires **2 Kastets**, with one placed on each field.

### Rewards

The rewards are always the same:

- **Death Machine** is summoned on top of the tower.
- The summoner receives **100 bonus points**.
- The summoner gains **5,000 × character level** experience points.

The summoned Death Machine also provides a hint pointing towards the **third item set**.

---

## Item Set 3 — Cloths

### Requirements

Each pull requires **3 Black Cloths** and **3 White Cloths**.

These are not individual pieces of cloth. Each Cloth is an item that can be created by combining **100 pieces of cloth**.

For more information about obtaining the required materials, refer to the **Systems / Tailoring** guide.

### Rewards

As of today, the rewards for this item set are **not publicly known**.

While **Black Cloth** can be sourced in large quantities from Tigermen, gathering the **300 pieces of white cloth** required to create the 3 White Cloths is considerably more time-consuming. It requires cutting items that are difficult to obtain.
