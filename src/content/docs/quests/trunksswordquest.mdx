---
title: Trunks Sword
description: Everything you need to know about getting the Trunks Sword.
---

The **Trunks Sword** spawns in a seemingly unreachable area after each server restart.

To get the sword, complete the following steps:

## 1. Get a Torch

1. Buy **Oil** from **NPC Helper** for **10 Credit Cards**.
2. Once you have **Oil**, it will point you toward the other pieces of the puzzle.
3. Get a **Piece of Wood** by:
   - Cutting down trees with an axe.
   - Cutting hand thorns.
4. Get **5 Piece of Cloth** of any random colors.
5. Use **Oil** on the **Piece of Wood** while carrying 5 **Piece of Cloth** to craft a **Torch**.

## 2. Light the Torch

1. After each server restart, click the bonfire on the ground level of **Tigerman Mountain** to get **Essence of Fire**.
2. You can get **3–9 Essence of Fire** each restart.
3. The bonfire dies down after its third use.
4. Use the **Torch** on **10 Essence of Fire** to light it.

:::caution[Server Restart]
**Essence of Fire** can only be obtained after a server restart.
:::

## 3. Burn the Bush

Use the lit **Torch** on the dry bush blocking the entrance to the room containing **Huge** and **Umami**.

:::tip[Torch]
The **Torch** is a permanent item and does not disappear when lit.

Burning a bush extinguishes the flame, so you will need to light the Torch again.
:::

## 4. Get the Crowbar

Kill **Huge** until they drop the **Crowbar**.

## 5. Open the Wall

Use the **Crowbar** on the crack in the wall.

## 6. Get the Trunks Sword

Walk up and pick up the **Trunks Sword**. You can pick multiple swords.
