---
title: Sleeping Ninja
description: How to access mysterious room with Sleeping Ninja.
---
To open the door you need to have pliers in your backpack. You can loot them from Commando.
Clicking on the barred door wakes up the Ninja. There are 5 great meats to be taken after each server restart.
Sleeping ninja wakes up and flees upstairs, where he can be killed for a chance to get loot associated with their loot table, including wooden boots.
