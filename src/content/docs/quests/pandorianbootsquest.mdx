---
title: Pandorian Boots Quest
description: Where to look for the chest containing the Pandorian boots.
---
Requirements: can do it at level 1 by dropping down the hole at the triceratops cave and accessing the tigermen mountain from there. Otherwise the door requires level 3 to open.

Monsters: wolves, Tigermen.

Location: Tigerman mountain, chest is on the 2nd level, where three Tigermen are, slightly hidden by the mountain.

Previously this quest gave the black dress as a reward.
