Bonus Points (BP) are a special currency that can be used for a variety of purposes throughout the game.
They are displayed in the **Skills** tab, below **Walking Speed**. The Bonus Point exchange module can be opened by clicking the **star icon** next to the number of available points.
Your current Bonus Point balance can also be checked using the `!bonus` command.
Bonus Points can be exchanged either through the exchange module or by using the `!bonus X` command. The exchange module is more user-friendly and provides an easier way to browse and select available exchanges.

## Sources of Bonus Points on Beginners Island
- **Levelling up** — Bonus Points are awarded at certain major level milestones. For example, 20 Bonus Points are awarded upon reaching level 20.
- **Daily check-ins** — 50 Bonus Points are awarded every 14 days of daily check-ins through the Daily Reward System.
- **Strong and Elite monsters** — Killing Strong and Elite monsters can reward Bonus Points. These monsters are identified by the skull icon next to their health bar.
- **Seasonal event monsters** — Killing certain monsters during seasonal events awards Bonus Points. Merman award 2 BP during the Summer Event. Other seasonal monsters include Ice Wolves during the Christmas Event and White Eggs during the Easter Event.
- **Seasonal event fishing** — Eating Carp caught during the Christmas Event awards 5 BP.
- **Easter Event Water Pistol** — Using the Water Pistol on Wet Monday (Easter Monday) can award 1 BP to the player splashed with water.
- **Bonus Point Boxes** — Bonus Point Boxes, obtainable from Surprise Boxes during the Summer Event, contain 100 BP.
- **Platform exchanges** — 2 Kastets can be exchanged for 100 BP on the Platform.
- **Lottery** — Every 7–9 hours, four random online players from different level brackets receive rewards. Each bracket has a 20% chance to award BP: 100 BP (1–300), 200 BP (301–600), 300 BP (601–900), 400 BP (901–1200), 500 BP (1201–1500), and 600 BP (1501+). A lottery notification appears 3 minutes before the draw.

## Using Bonus Points
Bonus Points can be exchanged for level and skill upgrades, Training Points, and various items.

### Level and Skills
Bonus Points are generally best spent on **level and skill upgrades**, especially when approaching their respective limits. You can exchange them for those as referenced:
- **Level** — up to **level 200**.
- **Ki Level** — up to **100**.
- **Strength** — up to **100**.
- **Sword Fighting** — up to **100**.
- **Attack Speed** — up to **100**.
- **Ki Blasting** — up to **100**.
- **Defence** — up to **100**.
- **Energy** — up to **100**.
- **Training Points** — up to **130**.

Depending on your priorities, it is generally recommended to use Bonus Points for **level upgrades only from around level 150 onward**.
For skills, **Energy** is a particularly good use of Bonus Points due to how difficult it can be to train. For other skills, consider using Bonus Points only when they reach the **high 80s or 90s**.
If your character trains a particular skill quickly, it is generally better to save your Bonus Points rather than spend them on that skill. For example, **Defence** and **Ki Level** are relatively fast to train and are usually poor choices for Bonus Point spending.
If you want to improve your training speed, prioritize **Attack Speed**. Keep in mind, however, that you can now progress from **Light Band to Thorns Band**, so reaching 100 Attack Speed takes considerably less time than it did when Light Band was the only option.
Conventional training may sometimes be inefficient. This can happen when a particular character gains a certain skill slowly, or when there are too few suitable monsters available for effective training. For example, **Energy** increases when a character is hit by a Ki attack. On Beginners Island, only **Death Machine, Huge, and Ninja** can be used for Energy training. With only a limited number of suitable monsters available, Energy training can therefore be inefficient.
