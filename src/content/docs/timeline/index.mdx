The history of Beginners Island presented here is based on changes documented in the game changelog and additional details found on the DBLOTS forums.
This timeline is not complete. The game has a long history, and a large amount of information from its earlier years is available across old changelog entries and forum discussions. As a result, some changes, events, and updates may be missing or may not yet have been documented here.
The timeline will be expanded as more historical information is reviewed and verified.
